TASK 15 — EXECUTIVE COMPANY-HEALTH SCORECARD

Selected KPIs:

1. M01 — Workforce Headcount
   Reason: Shows the current workforce size and capacity.

2. M04 — Monthly Attrition
   Reason: Measures employee retention and workforce stability.

3. M09 — Employer Cost
   Reason: Tracks the overall employer cost trend.

4. M11 — Overtime Share
   Reason: Highlights overtime usage and potential cost pressure.

5. M13 — Attendance Rate
   Reason: Measures workforce availability.

6. M16 — Revenue
   Reason: Tracks monthly business revenue.

7. M17 — Net Profit
   Reason: Shows the company's profitability.

8. M18 — Operating Margin
   Reason: Measures profitability relative to revenue.

Removed KPIs:

1. M02 — Hires
   Reason: Headcount already represents workforce size in the executive snapshot.

2. M03 — Exits
   Reason: Monthly attrition provides a more comparable employee-retention indicator.

3. M24 — Velocity
   Reason: Team-specific measure and not suitable for a company-wide executive comparison.

VALIDATION NOTES:

- All selected KPIs are sourced from their corresponding metric views.
- Department-level rates use the median of reportable department rates.
- Target compliance is calculated using configured department targets.
- No target is invented when no target is configured.
- Suppressed or null values are not treated as zero.
- Trends compare against the previous calendar month.
- Detailed KPI pages are linked from the executive scorecard.
- The scorecard is designed for A4 portrait printing.
